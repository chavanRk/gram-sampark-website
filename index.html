✦ Features

Everything your village needs,
in one app.

From farm prices to weather updates, Gram-Sampark is designed to bring the power of digital connectivity to every rural household.

🌤️

Live Weather

Get real-time weather forecasts, temperature, humidity, and wind data personalized for your location. Plan your farming activities with confidence.

📋

Government Schemes

Stay updated with the latest central and state government schemes. Apply for PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Soil Health Card, and more — all from one place.

🌾

Mandi Prices

Track real-time market prices for soybean, cotton, wheat, and other crops. Make informed selling decisions with daily price trends.

🔍

Explore Services

Discover local services — from agriculture equipment to healthcare, education, and financial services — all categorized and easy to find.

🤖

AI Assistant

Ask any question in Hindi, Marathi, or English. Our AI-powered assistant understands farming, health, government schemes, and daily life queries.

🚨

Emergency Services

Quick-dial emergency numbers for police, ambulance, fire brigade, and women's helpline. One tap when every second counts.

🏘️

Built for Villages

Designed with simplicity in mind for users in rural India. Available in Marathi and Hindi for seamless accessibility.

⚡

Firebase Powered

Real-time data synchronization ensures you always see the latest mandi prices, schemes, and service listings.

🛡️

Secure & Private

Firebase Authentication ensures your data is safe. Secure login with email and password — your information stays private.
